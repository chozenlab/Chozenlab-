## About Us

CHOZENLAB is a biomedical technology and life-sciences research company working at the intersection of computational biology, artificial intelligence, neuroscience, molecular biology and biomedical engineering.

We are interested in going beyond observing biological systems.

**We want to understand, model, reconstruct, modify and engineer them.**

Our vision is to use computational science and advanced technologies to expand what humanity can understand and accomplish in biology and medicine.

## Our Vision

### Building a Digital Paradise

A future where biological systems can be computationally represented, reconstructed, simulated and engineered.

We envision a world where:
- Biological systems can be modelled digitally.
- Neural networks can be reconstructed and simulated.
- DNA and proteins can be computationally analysed and engineered.
- Artificial intelligence accelerates biological discovery.
- Biomedical technologies create new possibilities for healthcare.

## Research Direction

One of our long-term ambitions is to contribute to the computational reconstruction of biological intelligence.

Starting with existing connectomics research, we are interested in advancing the understanding of neural structures, synaptic connections and neuronal dynamics.

Our research direction includes:

1. Neural reconstruction.
2. Connectome analysis.
3. Computational neural modelling.
4. Biological intelligence simulation.
5. Digital representations of complex biological systems.

The long-term ambition is to contribute to whole-brain emulation research.

## Our Philosophy

We believe that the future of biological science lies in the integration of computation, engineering and experimental biology.

We don't want to limit ourselves to observing what nature has already created.

We want to understand how it works, explore how it can be modelled and develop technologies that allow us to engineer new possibilities.

**Observe. Understand. Reconstruct. Engineer.**
